Frequently Asked Questions

How does Bookify work?
Bookify is an AI-powered reading assistant that recommends books and research papers based on your interests. Simply search or chat with the AI, and it will suggest relevant titles. You can add books to your collection, track your reading progress, take notes, and access books through sources like Internet Archive, Open Library, or Amazon for purchase.
Is Bookify free to use?
Bookify offers a free plan with basic features as well as a Pro plan with enhanced capabilities for more serious readers, students, and researchers. The Free plan gives you access to core features with some limitations, while the Pro plan offers unlimited AI queries, more search results, and full access to archives and research papers.
Can I download books directly from Bookify?
Bookify provides links to legitimate sources where you can purchase or borrow books. These include Open Library, Internet Archive, and Amazon. We don't host book files directly but connect you to the best available options, although you can download select research papers directly from Bookify if available.
How does the AI assistant work?
The AI assistant uses natural language processing to analyze inputs related to user preferences, mood, titles, authors, genre, etc. It surfs through our vast database of books, research papers, and archives from multiple sources, including Open Library, Internet Archive, Semantic Scholar, etc., to recommend relevant titles related to your preferences. Users are free to interact with it through chat for dynamic recommendations or perform direct searches, also available in our quick search feature.
How do I track my reading progress?
Once you have added books or papers to your collection, you can update your reading status, mark pages read, take notes, etc. Your books will always be available in your collection once added, and are free to be modified or deleted at your own will. If you don't see a specific book being recommended, you are free to add your own custom titles in the book tracker feature.
